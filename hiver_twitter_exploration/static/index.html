End-to-End System Topology (Phases 1–10)

A production-ready conversational customer support system designed with mathematical rigor to eliminate data leakage, prevent conversational hallucinations, and prioritize brand safety.

01

Data Exploration

Reconstructs parent tweet pointers (in_response_to_tweet_id) across 2.81M tweets in twcs.csv to isolate 106,646 authentic @AppleSupport interaction pairs.

02

Anti-Leakage Splitting

Groups multi-turn threads by conversation_root_or_group_id (80% Train, 10% Val, 10% Test) to guarantee 0% cross-partition conversational data leakage.

03

8-Class Taxonomy

Inductively mines recurring failure modes on training inquiries to establish 8 actionable intents, avoiding generic e-commerce categories.

04 & 05

Weak Labels & Baselines

Generates transparent silver labels exclusively for training/val data and compares against Majority-Class and Regex heuristic baselines.

06

Explainable Classifier

Sublinear TF-IDF (1–2 n-grams, 30,000 features) + balanced Logistic Regression with softmax probabilities and low-confidence human review gating.

07

Train-Only Retrieval

In-memory sparse cosine similarity over 82,063 unique historical training inquiries. Strict lexical similarity bands prevent false confidence.

08

Safe Reply Drafting

Deterministic template adaptation with regex safety detectors. Prohibits credential requests, fake URLs, or fabricated promises.

09

Risk-Aware Routing

Prioritizes safety: 100% of sensitive domains (passwords, refunds, repairs) escalate to humans. Only low-risk, high-confidence queries qualify for auto-handling.
